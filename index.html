Beyond country totals

Population changes the comparison

A smaller country can emit less overall while having higher
emissions per person. National totals alone do not show this
difference.

The next section compares emissions across populations of different sizes.

05

Power and transport lead Malaysia's emissions growth

Malaysia contributes 0.62% of global emissions. Its total nearly quadrupled since 1990, driven by power and transport.

For Malaysia: power and transport lead its emissions growth.
Its small global share contrasts with above-average emissions per person.
